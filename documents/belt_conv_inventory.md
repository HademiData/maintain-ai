# 14. MAINTENANCE PLANNING RULE

Before creating a work order, the maintenance planner should consider:

1. Equipment condition
2. Failure severity
3. Safety risk
4. Production impact
5. Required spare parts
6. Required consumables
7. Required manpower
8. Required tools
9. Estimated maintenance cost
10. Equipment availability
11. Previous maintenance history
12. Required isolation and safety controls

Inventory availability and actual prices should always be verified
against the organization's current inventory and procurement system.
