BELT CONVEYOR MAINTENANCE MANUAL

Asset: Belt Conveyor BC-01
Department: Maintenance Operations

1. PURPOSE
This manual provides maintenance guidance for Belt Conveyor BC-01
used for continuous material transportation.

2. KEY COMPONENTS

- Conveyor belt
- Drive motor
- Gearbox
- Drive pulley
- Tail pulley
- Idler rollers
- Bearings
- Belt scraper
- Take-up system
- Emergency stop system

1. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

Daily:

- Inspect belt condition for cuts, tears, and abnormal wear.
- Check for belt misalignment.
- Inspect for unusual noise or vibration.
- Check for material buildup around pulleys and idlers.
- Verify emergency stop devices are accessible.

Weekly:

- Inspect idlers for abnormal rotation and damage.
- Check belt tension and tracking.
- Inspect pulley surfaces.
- Check gearbox and motor for abnormal noise or overheating.
- Inspect belt scraper condition.

Monthly:

- Inspect bearings for wear and overheating.
- Check gearbox oil condition.
- Inspect drive and tail pulleys.
- Inspect take-up system.
- Check structural supports and fasteners.

1. COMMON FAILURE RISKS

- Belt misalignment
- Belt damage
- Idler bearing failure
- Pulley wear
- Gearbox overheating
- Motor overheating
- Inadequate belt tension
- Material buildup

1. MAINTENANCE OBJECTIVE
The maintenance team should identify defects early, prioritize
high-risk conditions, and prevent unplanned conveyor downtime.
