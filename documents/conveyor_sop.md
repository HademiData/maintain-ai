BELT CONVEYOR MAINTENANCE SOP

Asset: BC-01

1. SAFETY
Before maintenance:

- Stop the conveyor.
- Isolate the electrical power supply.
- Apply lockout/tagout procedures.
- Verify zero-energy state.
- Use appropriate PPE.
- Prevent unauthorized access to the work area.

1. MAINTENANCE WORKFLOW

Step 1: Review the previous maintenance history.

Step 2: Inspect the conveyor and identify defects.

Step 3: Assess the severity and operational impact.

Step 4: Create or update the maintenance work order.

Step 5: Assign required personnel, tools, and spare parts.

Step 6: Complete the maintenance task.

Step 7: Test the equipment after maintenance.

Step 8: Record findings and close the work order.

1. WORK ORDER PRIORITY

HIGH:
Conditions that could cause immediate safety risks,
major equipment damage, or significant production downtime.

MEDIUM:
Conditions that could reduce equipment reliability or
cause deterioration if not addressed.

LOW:
Minor defects that do not currently affect operation
but should be monitored or corrected during planned maintenance.

1. PLANNING CONSIDERATIONS
Maintenance planners should consider:

- Equipment condition
- Failure risk
- Production requirements
- Required manpower
- Tools and spare parts
- Safety requirements
- Maintenance history
